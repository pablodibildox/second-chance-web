1. What Second Chance is

Second Chance is a free, ad-free app that shows “zu verschenken” (free giveaway) boxes left on the street on a map. Users photograph boxes, tag what’s inside by category, and keep the map current by confirming whether a box still has items. The app only points to where a box is; it is not involved in handing over items and is not a contracting party between users.

2. Free, no availability guarantee

Use is free. There are no in-app purchases and no advertising. There is no claim to availability of the app or any feature. The service may be changed, limited or discontinued at any time.

3. Accounts

On first launch an anonymous account is created in the background; with it you can use the map, confirm boxes, record takes and create saved searches. To post a box yourself, “Sign in with Apple” is required. You are responsible for protecting your device and access. Data processing details are in the privacy policy.

4. Posting rules

You confirm these rules in the app before posting for the first time. When you post a box:

  • Photograph only the box. No people, no vehicles, no number plates, no doorbell/name plates in the picture.
  • Only genuine items that are free to give away — no sales offers, no prices.
  • Nothing dangerous or illegal, and nothing whose passing on is prohibited.
  • Only boxes in public space that you have actually seen on site.

You are responsible for the photos you upload and warrant that you may publish them and infringe no third-party rights. The app automatically masks detected people, vehicles and plate-like text on the device before upload; this automatic masking does not relieve you of your own duty of care.
