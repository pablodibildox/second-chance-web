Report content

Using the app? Report a box or photo in the app via the report function (⋯ on the box). A privacy report hides the photo immediately.

Not an app user and want a photo or box taken down — e.g. because you or your vehicle appear in it? Use the contact form below (notice-and-action under Art. 16 of the Digital Services Act). You can also email us.

Your report goes directly to our server function at Supabase (EU, Frankfurt); no cookies are set and no third parties are embedded. We review every report (target: within 48 hours) and reply to the email address you give. More in the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy.
